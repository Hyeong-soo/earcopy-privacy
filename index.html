EarCopy Privacy Policy

Updated for EarCopy 1.1: September 15, 2026

Overview

EarCopy ("the App") is a music practice tool developed by Hyeong-soo Kim. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

Data Collection

EarCopy does not operate an account system. Optional usage statistics are described below; they do not include your music library or the contents of your practice sessions. Imported audio, saved lyrics, bookmarks, and practice settings are stored on your device. Online music playback and lyrics lookup contact the services described below.

What the App Accesses

  • Apple Music Library: If you grant permission, EarCopy accesses your Apple Music library to browse and play songs for practice. Playback and artwork use Apple's services. A selected song's metadata may also be used to find lyrics as described below.
  • Local Audio Files: You may import audio files from the Files app. These files are stored locally within the app's sandbox.
  • Lyrics Lookup: When lyrics are needed for an imported or selected song, EarCopy may automatically send its title, artist, album name (when available), and duration to LRCLIB over HTTPS. Retrying a lookup also sends this metadata. EarCopy does not upload your audio file, imported lyrics, or bookmarks to LRCLIB. Like other internet services, LRCLIB receives connection information such as your IP address.

In-App Purchases

Lyrics Practice Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. The App checks verified StoreKit transactions on your device to unlock features, restore purchases, and reflect refunds or revocations. The developer does not receive your payment card details or Apple account password. The App does not send purchase history to a developer-operated server.

Data Storage

Song metadata, saved lyrics, bookmarks, and practice settings are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. EarCopy does not synchronize this library to a developer-operated cloud service. If you use the Apple Watch companion, playback state and bookmark names are shared with your paired Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework.

Third-Party Services

EarCopy uses TelemetryDeck only with your agreement, as described below. It does not use advertising services, advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, or session recording.

Optional Apple Music functionality uses Apple's services. Online lyrics are provided by LRCLIB, a separate service whose processing is outside the developer's control. You can also import an LRC file or enter lyrics yourself; saved lyrics remain available on your device.

Optional Usage Statistics

Sharing is off by default. After practice, an optional invitation explains the choice; you can also choose it in Settings. Refusing does not restrict any features. With your agreement, EarCopy sends feature usage, playback and lyrics lookup outcomes, coarse loading-time ranges, app version, operating system name and major version, and a random session identifier over HTTPS to TelemetryDeck GmbH, an analytics provider in Europe. Its hosting documentation describes processing in Germany and the Netherlands. The purpose is to improve functionality and reliability.

EarCopy does not send song titles, artists, lyrics, audio, filenames, bookmark text, contact details, purchase history, raw error messages, or persistent user/device identifiers for analytics. The random session identifier changes when the app returns from the background or sharing is changed, and is not saved to disk. TelemetryDeck necessarily receives connection information such as an IP address and states that it does not retain IP addresses.

Turning sharing off in Settings stops new collection, cancels in-flight requests where possible, and deletes unsent events. It does not recall data already received by the service. TelemetryDeck currently has no fixed deletion schedule for analytics events, including after an account is closed. For current processing and retention details, see TelemetryDeck's privacy information. Contact us at the address below about this choice.

Apple may separately share aggregated App Store usage, sales, and diagnostic information with the developer under Apple's policies and your device's analytics sharing settings. EarCopy's optional sharing switch controls only the TelemetryDeck events above.

Reviews and Feedback

EarCopy keeps limited practice totals and review-request dates on your device to avoid interrupting practice or requesting reviews too often. These counters are not uploaded. You can disable automatic requests in Settings. Apple controls whether its native request appears; EarCopy cannot determine whether you wrote a review or what rating you gave. The feedback link opens your mail app without attaching files or sending a message automatically. If you send an email, the developer receives the contact details and content you choose to include to respond to your request.

Children's Privacy

EarCopy does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected in the app or on this page.
